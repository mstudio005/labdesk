2. LABDESK IS A TOOL, NOT A DOCTOR
LabDesk is not a medical device and gives no medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It prints the values that people in your lab type in or attach from a machine, and marks them HIGH, LOW or CRIT by comparing them with the normal ranges that are stored in the program.
- The lab, and its pathologist, are fully responsible for every result and every report that is approved and handed out.
- The normal ranges, units, methods and formulas in the standard test list are a starting point. They can be wrong for your kit, machine, method or patients. Check every range and formula against your kit insert and your own standards before you use it.
- Always check a result before you approve it. A doctor must approve a report before it is printed.

4. PRIVACY: WHAT LABDESK COLLECTS AND SENDS
LabDesk does not collect your data. It has no account system and no server of its own, and the author does not receive your patient data.
- Stored on your computer only: patients, orders, results, payments, users, settings and backups.
- Update check: when LabDesk starts, and every few hours, it asks GitHub for the latest version number and downloads a newer installer if there is one. GitHub can see that request, as with any download. You can turn this off in Settings > About.
- Email: switched off until your Admin turns it on. When you press EMAIL, the report is sent through the mail service you chose. That service and the patient's email provider can see the message and file.
- WhatsApp: LabDesk opens WhatsApp Web in your browser. WhatsApp handles the message under its own terms. You press Send yourself.
- LabDesk Bridge browser extension (optional): talks only to LabDesk on the same computer, collects nothing and sends nothing to the author.
- Problem reports: if the program closes unexpectedly you can choose to send a short report by email. Patient names and numbers are removed. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
- The website https://labdesk.mutaib.me has its own privacy policy.
