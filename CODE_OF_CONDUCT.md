# Code of Conduct

We want LabDesk's issues, discussions and pull requests to be a respectful place for everyone, including people who run small labs and are not programmers.

## What we expect

- Be polite and patient. Assume good intentions.
- Give and accept criticism of the work, not of the person.
- Keep patient privacy first. Never share real patient data.
- Stay on topic and help others find the answer.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Spam or advertising unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem write to **syedmutaibnazir@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
